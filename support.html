Privacy policy

Account deletion

You can delete your account in the BreathBoss app by opening:

Home → Quit Settings → Delete Account

Deleting your account removes account access and may anonymize prior community content where deletion would affect conversation integrity.

Community safety

BreathBoss includes report and hide options in supported community features. Users can report concerning behavior or hide other users from supported spaces such as community chat and support interactions.
