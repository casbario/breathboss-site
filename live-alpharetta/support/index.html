Privacy policy

Do I need an account to browse?

You can open Live and Places without signing in. Use Sign in with Apple for check-ins or business access.

How do I list my business?

Sign in, open your account, and choose Apply for business access. An administrator reviews the details before a listing becomes public.

How do I report a listing or request deletion?

Email support with the listing name or type of account request. Include only the details needed to identify the issue.
