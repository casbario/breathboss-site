Live Alpharetta privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

App support

Browsing and signing in

Live and Places can be browsed without an account. When you sign in, Apple and Firebase Authentication process an account identifier and may provide a name and email. Account names and emails are kept in private records.

Check-ins and public activity

A check-in records the place you select and when you check in. These are self-reported visits. Public activity uses a separate identifier and contributes to activity counts for the place.

Business applications and posts

Applications include business and representative contact details for administrator review. Approved listing details, specials, events, and photos submitted for publication can be seen by other users. Private application contact details are kept separate from the listing.

Storage, expiry, and requests

Cloud Firestore stores profiles, applications, check-ins, posts, and submitted post photos. An expired check-in or post can disappear from view while its record remains in Firestore. Email support to request access, correction, or deletion.

Contacting support

If you email Rhino Builds, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so we can respond. You can ask about access, correction, or removal at the address on this page.
