Privacy policy

How do I change a target?

Open Settings to adjust your daily carbs, protein, fat, and optional weight goal.

Can I correct an earlier entry?

Open the history editor and choose the date to change. Clearing the weight field removes that date’s weight entry.

Can support recover my log?

Entries are stored on your iPhone. The support team has no account copy of your log to restore.
