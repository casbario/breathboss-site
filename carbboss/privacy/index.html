CarbBoss privacy policy

CarbBoss stores your nutrition and weight log on your iPhone.

Effective November 20, 2026

Contact Rhino Builds

Email a privacy questionRhinoBuildsSupport@gmail.com
App support

The information you enter

CarbBoss stores dated carb, protein, fat, and weight entries along with your targets. It uses these values to display totals, charts, and progress toward your goals.

Where it is stored

The app uses local device storage and preferences. It does not require an account or send your nutrition and weight history to a Rhino Builds backend. Copies in device backups depend on your Apple backup settings.

Editing and removing entries

Use the history editor to correct daily records. Deleting the app and its data removes its local records from that device. Existing device backups are managed separately through Apple.

Contacting support

If you email Rhino Builds, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so we can respond. You can ask about access, correction, or removal at the address on this page.
