Privacy policy

Can I track two goals?

Yes. In Goal settings, choose one or both habits and a separate quit or reduction goal for each.

Who can see my check-ins?

Your private check-ins are kept separate from community posts. Content you choose to share in a community interaction is visible to its participants.

How do I ask about removing data?

Email support and tell us whether you used a device-only journal or an account. Leave out the contents of your journal.
