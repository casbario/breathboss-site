ResistBoss privacy policy

ResistBoss is for adults. Its private recovery journal and optional shared features handle different information.

App support

Private goals and check-ins

The journal records the behaviors you choose, your goals, dates, and check-ins such as urges, resisted urges, or setbacks. These entries are used to calculate progress and are separate from your public alias and community posts.

Device and account storage

In device-only mode, the journal is stored in protected app files on your iPhone. In an account-connected session, plans and check-ins are stored under your account in Cloud Firestore. Availability of online features depends on the build.

Shared features and providers

Account-connected features use Sign in with Apple, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions. They process account identifiers, aliases, and the messages, activity, or reports you submit. Other participants can see the alias and content you choose to share.

Removing data

Account deletion can remove or anonymize account-linked records, including shared activity. A device-only journal is removed from that device when you delete the app and its data. Contact support for help with either mode.

Contacting support

If you email Rhino Builds, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so we can respond. You can ask about access, correction, or removal at the address on this page.
