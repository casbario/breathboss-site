BreathBoss Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BreathBoss handles information used to provide account access, progress tracking, community features, SOS support, battles, reports, and related app services.

Effective date: July 9, 2026

Contact

For privacy questions or support requests, contact RhinoBuildsSupport@gmail.com.

Information we collect

BreathBoss may collect information you provide or create in the app, including your account identifier, alias, public profile information, quit date, progress settings, streak information, health milestone progress, Campfire messages, SOS interactions, battle activity, community quote submissions, reports, and hidden-user preferences.

BreathBoss uses Sign in with Apple and Firebase Authentication for account access. If you sign in with Apple, Apple may provide an account identifier used to authenticate you. BreathBoss does not require your real name for community features and encourages users to use an alias.

Firebase services

BreathBoss uses Firebase Authentication for sign-in, Firestore for app data storage, and Firebase Functions for server-side app operations such as protected community, SOS, battle, reporting, and account-related workflows.

The app includes Firebase Analytics. Analytics settings and data handling may depend on the app configuration and Firebase services. BreathBoss uses Firebase services to operate and improve the app experience, not to sell personal information.

Community features

Community spaces such as Campfire, SOS support, battle accountability, and community quotes may show your alias or public profile information to other users. Messages, interactions, battle activity, quote submissions, reports, and hide preferences may be stored so the app can provide these features and maintain community safety.

Reports and hidden users

When you report another user, BreathBoss may store information about the report, the reported user, related content, timestamps, and your account identifier so the report can be reviewed or acted on. When you hide another user, BreathBoss stores that preference so supported app areas can reduce interactions with that user.

How we use information

BreathBoss uses information to provide account access, sync your progress, calculate streak and milestone state, operate community features, manage SOS and battle interactions, process quote submissions, support user safety, troubleshoot issues, and maintain the app.

Account deletion and retention

You can delete your account in the app by opening:

Home → Quit Settings → Delete Account

Account deletion removes account access and deletes or anonymizes account-related data where appropriate. Some prior community content may remain in anonymized form where deletion would affect conversation integrity, safety records, reports, SOS interactions, battles, quote history, or other shared app records.

Security

BreathBoss relies on Apple platform security, Firebase Authentication, Firestore security rules, Firebase Functions, and HTTPS/TLS networking provided by Apple and Firebase. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but BreathBoss uses reasonable technical measures for the type of app data it handles.

Children's privacy

BreathBoss is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through BreathBoss, contact support so the issue can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
