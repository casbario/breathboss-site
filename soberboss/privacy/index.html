SoberBoss privacy policy

How SoberBoss handles sobriety tracking, community features, and support.

App support

Recovery and account information

SoberBoss uses your sobriety date, spending estimate, streaks, and challenge progress to show your milestones. Sign in with Apple and Firebase Authentication provide an account identifier. Your chosen alias identifies you to other players.

Community activity

Messages, SOS interactions, battle moves and results, profile information, and quotes are processed to provide community features. Other participants can see the alias and content you post. Reports and hide preferences help moderate these spaces.

Service providers

Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions support accounts, stored records, and community actions. The app includes Firebase Analytics, which may process app usage and device information depending on its configuration.

Account and data removal

Use Home, Recovery Settings, then Delete Account. The deletion workflow removes account data and can anonymize earlier shared content. Some safety records and content involving other participants may remain.

Contacting support

If you email Rhino Builds, we receive the email address and information you choose to send so we can respond. You can ask about access, correction, or removal at the address on this page.
