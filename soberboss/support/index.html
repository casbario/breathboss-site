How do I change my recovery date?

Open Home and choose Recovery Settings to update your sobriety date and spending estimate.

How do I delete my account?

Open Home, choose Recovery Settings, then Delete Account. Follow the confirmation prompts.

Where can I report a concern?

Use the report or hide action in the relevant community screen. For follow-up, email support with the screen name and approximate time.
